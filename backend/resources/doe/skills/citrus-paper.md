# Citrus Paper

Use for learning, community activity, approachable products or a warm practical story. Build the visual world from cream paper, lime/olive layers and one coral accent, with shallow, believable paper thickness and directional shadows. The image should shape an edge or explain an object; it should not become a framed illustration pasted onto the slide. Keep paper grain subtle, shadows local and the reading field calm. Avoid confetti, generic flowers, glossy plastic and every possible color at once.

The registered theme uses cream #FFF4C4, deep olive headings #20320F and primary green #4D680F. Fredoka leads English headings, Alexandria carries body text, and MiSans handles Chinese. The muted olive workspace supports the brighter page. Rounded type and soft paper silhouettes provide warmth; native evidence stays crisp. This is an original DOE direction informed by the user's paper-sculpture learning references, not an official Gamma theme.

Use `theme=yrkie-citrus` for a new deck unless the user selected another theme. On an existing deck, read its current revision and use SetDeckTheme; do not silently replace the user's choice. Ask about missing density, audience or brand assets through the available question tool before production; respect an already answered brief and outline confirmation. Theme roles supply fonts and colors. Do not invent authorable CSS, absolute coordinates, font fields or custom image masks.

Keep real dates, counts and percentages in editable text; a component ID containing numbered does not require decorative numerals. Use one dominant visual and related support, not several competing diagrams. A single metric plus two reasons can be stronger than three full panels. Do not default to one title component or a page of bullets.

High: prioritize the complete evidence in an open chart/table or clearly grouped material; reduce picture footprint before type. Preserve facts, units, bases, periods, sources, alternatives and uncertainty. Do not shrink a whole block, omit facts or repeat every chart value in prose. For a donut with named categories, normally set showLegend=true explicitly, including compact variants. ## Images are part of the composition

Carry image art direction throughout the deck, not only on the cover. Choose subject imagery that explains the brief, atmospheric scenes that establish mood, or page-surface artwork around editable content. A well-designed decorative background is a valid inner-page visual; it need not contain a subject photo. Preserve regular visual presence and varied page silhouettes without a fixed image percentage or asset count. Dense evidence pages may remain plain. Respect text/data-only requests and factual-image restrictions; illustration and atmosphere are not documentary evidence.

Plan the role in the Outline with generateimg, ratio and boolean removeBackground. Direct GenerateImage accepts prompt, ratio and optional removeBackground: no style, negativePrompt or aspectRatio fields. Resolve all bracketed placeholders before calling. After generation call ReadImage on the exact returned reference, then place that exact slideReference. A prose promise or a link alone is not an image. Keep all titles, values, chart labels and diagram relationships native and editable.

The default Slide fill is `#FFF4C4`; the outer workspace and card surfaces are different. A matched image with removeBackground=false must request this actual page color uniformly at every joining edge, without edge shadows, texture, gradients, vignette, white mat or frame. An intentional scene photo may retain its setting. A color prompt does not guarantee a pixel-perfect seam. For an isolated complete subject use removeBackground=true: the host adds the matting preset and removes the background; do not write green-screen instructions yourself. Use fit=contain, and inspect real alpha/edge quality rather than claiming transparency from a prompt. Keep natural green material green.

For full backgrounds, inspect the actual quiet zone before setting contentWidth/contentAlign. contentWidth is a fraction of the text safe area, not of image pixels. textSurface=none affects text primitives only; collections retain their reading surfaces. foreground=light makes direct text light; foreground=dark retains theme text colors. Do not combine a full background with edge-image-layout. A normal content image does not require a full-page image or a frame.

## Cutout quality and background choice

For cutout quality, this guidance supersedes earlier illustrative removal settings and replacement-count advice. Green foliage, sage materials and green reflections can overlap the host's green key: a risk, not guaranteed failure. For green paper layers, including the earlier isolated-object recipe; retain their intended color, prefer `removeBackground=false`: use a uniform background matching the actual visible slide fill at placement (theme default `#FFF4C4`), or an intentional real scene. Preserve the subject's colors.

Prefer `removeBackground=true` for complete, clearly separated subjects whose visible colors contrast with the host's green key; avoid forcing green subjects through this route. Do not request a textured background, floor, scene or cast/drop shadow; the object's material texture and form shading may remain. The host supplies matting instructions.

Before placement, use `ReadImage` on the exact returned asset. Inspect speckles, gray/green halos, cropped tips and damaged edges at the intended scale. Do not enlarge or place a visibly dirty cutout. Use a matched-background `false` alternative or diagnose the observed defect before targeted regeneration through the normal tool, within existing tool limits and the user's authorized scope. A successful call does not prove clean edges or correct alpha; never claim an unverified cleanup.
