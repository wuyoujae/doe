## Cutout quality and background choice

For cutout quality, this guidance supersedes earlier illustrative removal settings and replacement-count advice. Green foliage, sage materials and green reflections can overlap the host's green key: a risk, not guaranteed failure. For mint-colored product forms or green reflections in translucent material, prefer `removeBackground=false`: use a uniform background matching the actual visible slide fill at placement (theme default `#21162F`), or an intentional real scene. Preserve the subject's colors.

Prefer `removeBackground=true` for complete, clearly separated subjects whose visible colors contrast with the host's green key; avoid forcing green subjects through this route. Do not request a textured background, floor, scene or cast/drop shadow; the object's material texture and form shading may remain. The host supplies matting instructions.
