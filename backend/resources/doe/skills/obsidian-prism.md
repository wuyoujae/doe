# Obsidian Prism

Use for precise product narratives, instruments, creative technology or a focused launch. Start from neutral near-black, calm white type and disciplined spacing. Make iridescence a property of one real-looking glass or coated-metal object: a thin spectral rim, a selective reflection, a contained refracted patch. Do not fill the slide with rainbow fog, purple ribbons, glowing circuits, stars or cobalt gradients. The identity is physical material under controlled light, not generic futuristic scenery.

The registered theme uses #141417 pages, #F5F4EF headlines, #E1E3E9 body text and #D4D2FF accents. Alexandria supplies English headings/body and MiSans supplies Chinese. Card surfaces are quiet charcoal, and the outer stage is deeper neutral black. Pale violet, mint, rose, blue and amber chart colors remain distinct against the page. Keep photographs color-accurate; tint only generated illustrative material. This is an original direction, not a Gamma template or a claim of a product's real capabilities.

Select page roles from the approved argument. Vary dominant geometry and composition across nearby pages using the current selection policy; a role does not prescribe one component.

Keep all titles, values, chart labels and diagram relationships native and editable.

The default Slide fill is `#141417`; the outer workspace and card surfaces are different. A matched image with removeBackground=false must request this actual page color uniformly at every joining edge, without edge shadows, texture, gradients, vignette, white mat or frame. An intentional scene photo may retain its setting. A color prompt does not guarantee a pixel-perfect seam. For an isolated complete subject use removeBackground=true: the host adds the matting preset and removes the background; do not write green-screen instructions yourself. Use fit=contain, and inspect real alpha/edge quality rather than claiming transparency from a prompt. Keep natural green material green.

For full backgrounds, inspect the actual quiet zone before setting contentWidth/contentAlign. contentWidth is a fraction of the text safe area, not of image pixels. textSurface=none affects text primitives only; collections retain their reading surfaces. foreground=light makes direct text light; foreground=dark retains theme text colors. ## Cutout quality and background choice

For cutout quality, this guidance supersedes earlier illustrative removal settings and replacement-count advice. Green foliage, sage materials and green reflections can overlap the host's green key: a risk, not guaranteed failure. For green iridescent highlights or mint-colored product surfaces, prefer `removeBackground=false`: use a uniform background matching the actual visible slide fill at placement (theme default `#141417`), or an intentional real scene. Preserve the subject's colors.

Prefer `removeBackground=true` for complete, clearly separated subjects whose visible colors contrast with the host's green key; avoid forcing green subjects through this route. Do not request a textured background, floor, scene or cast/drop shadow; the object's material texture and form shading may remain. The host supplies matting instructions.

Before placement, use `ReadImage` on the exact returned asset. Inspect speckles, gray/green halos, cropped tips and damaged edges at the intended scale. Do not enlarge or place a visibly dirty cutout. Use a matched-background `false` alternative or diagnose the observed defect before targeted regeneration through the normal tool, within existing tool limits and the user's authorized scope. A successful call does not prove clean edges or correct alpha; never claim an unverified cleanup.
