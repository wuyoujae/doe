### Image prompt A: background with text space

`ratio=16:9`, `removeBackground=false`.

> Create a restrained technical atmosphere image on a deep navy-blue field. `ratio=3:4`, `removeBackground=false`.

> Photograph one unbranded precision mechanical assembly on a dark-blue laboratory surface. ## Check the result

Choose low, medium or high density from the actual content, not the desire to fill a canvas. Keep required statements, values, alternatives, qualifiers and relationships before trimming repetition. If a dense page cannot remain legible, reorganize within the agreed page count or request a scope change; do not omit facts or shrink them into unreadable notes.

A background's quiet area must match the native text region: `contentWidth` is a fraction of the safe-area width, not a percentage of the picture. Set `contentAlign` and inspect all text lines. `textSurface=none` removes reading panels only for title-group, section-heading and text-paragraph. `foreground=light` gives direct text a pale color; `dark` keeps theme colors. Other components retain their own surfaces. Use an edge split if text and subject cannot coexist. No full image background is used with an edge-image-layout.

Check the rendered page when a preview is available: hierarchy, complete content, readable labels, real contrast and crop. Syntax success is not a visual pass. Do not claim pixels were inspected when only Markdown or a URL was read. One strong component can be enough; multiple components should have distinct, related jobs. Avoid repeated decoration and repeated conclusions.

> Create an original technical presentation background, landscape 16:9, with a uniform deep blue #102D50 center. Reserve normalized x=0.06-0.94, y=0.06-0.91 as a completely quiet reading field. No arrows, dimensions, units, connected nodes, circuitry, diagrams, readable scales, text, numbers, logo, icons, content cards, chart, UI or completed slide. ## Cutout quality and background choice

For cutout quality, this guidance supersedes earlier illustrative removal settings and replacement-count advice. Green foliage, sage materials and green reflections can overlap the host's green key: a risk, not guaranteed failure. For green instrument parts, indicators or coatings, prefer `removeBackground=false`: use a uniform background matching the actual visible slide fill at placement (theme default `#102D50`), or an intentional real scene. Preserve the subject's colors.

Prefer `removeBackground=true` for complete, clearly separated subjects whose visible colors contrast with the host's green key; avoid forcing green subjects through this route. Do not request a textured background, floor, scene or cast/drop shadow; the object's material texture and form shading may remain. The host supplies matting instructions.

Before placement, use `ReadImage` on the exact returned asset. Inspect speckles, gray/green halos, cropped tips and damaged edges at the intended scale. Do not enlarge or place a visibly dirty cutout. Use a matched-background `false` alternative or diagnose the observed defect before targeted regeneration through the normal tool, within existing tool limits and the user's authorized scope. A successful call does not prove clean edges or correct alpha; never claim an unverified cleanup.
